FINAL LOOP — REMEMBRANCE (chapter 04)

Press Tab to check fragments; earlier collected fragments still count.
1. PHOTOGRAPH: Floor 01, starting/west corridor, first bedroom on your right
   when walking forward from spawn.
   Look inside for EXAMINE THE PHOTOGRAPH and press E.
2. EVACUATION LIST: Floor 02, central hall north of the stairs.
   Find the desk under EVACUATION REGISTER, look at the list and press E.
3. WRISTBAND: Floor 04, west corridor (same side as the starting corridor),
   northernmost bedroom.
   Examine the bedside table beside the hospital bed with E.
4. FIRE EXIT: Floor 04, east corridor, far/north end under EXIT.
   Press E on the door, move through, then press E at LEAVE THROUGH
   THE FIRE DOOR. With all three fragments, the final choice appears.
5. Choose LET HIM FINISH for the acceptance ending.

Stairs: take the flight that rises ahead, cross its landing, then turn
back and climb the other flight. Repeat per floor.

You do not need to kill or disarm everyone. The final-loop exit begins
unlocked: restoring the Floor 02 circuit again is not required.
INSIST IT WAS NECESSARY gives the alternate ending; Reconsider returns
to the choice.

0.9.1: Press E directly on the fire exit from the corridor. You no longer
need to walk behind its door. Three fragments are still required.
